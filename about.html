Our Mission
What is Lexbility?

Lexbility is a free legal resource platform built for Indian law students and practicing advocates.

Legal knowledge is a fundamental right — yet it often remains obscured by complexity, paywalls, and access barriers. Lexbility exists to change that. We simplify Bare Acts, curate the best legal resources, build practical guides, and make the Indian legal ecosystem navigable for everyone from first-year law students to senior advocates.

Whether you are preparing for CLAT, appearing in court for the first time, or looking for the right bare act at 11 PM before a hearing — Lexbility has you covered.

The Person Behind Lexbility
A Mission for Access
Myself Prashant Pandey a law student at the Campus Law Centre, University of Delhi Batch 2024-27, and the founder of Lexbility.

My journey into law is driven by a singular conviction: legal knowledge is a fundamental right, yet it often remains obscured by complexity and access barriers. Through Lexbility, I have channeled my passion for legal research and technology to bridge this gap — creating a repository that simplifies Bare Acts, case laws, and procedural requirements for students and practitioners alike.
Professional Philosophy
My approach to law is rooted in the intersection of traditional legal research and modern efficiency. Whether it is through my work as a legal intern in criminal and civil drafting, or my service as the Joint Secretary of the Campus Law Centre Students' Union, I prioritize precision, ethical practice, and the proactive application of legal knowledge.
What I'm Looking For
I am currently seeking mentorship and opportunities to contribute to high-stakes litigation and legal consultancy. I am particularly interested in chambers where technology and rigorous research are utilized to deliver exceptional client outcomes.

If you believe in democratizing legal access, or would like to discuss my work or a potential collaboration — please reach out. I would love to connect.
