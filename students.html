How to Apply for Internships
Learn the exact process to secure law internships professionally.

Step-by-Step Process

1. Decide Your Target

  • Litigation Chambers
  • Corporate Law Firms
  • NGOs
  • Courts & Judges
  • Policy Organizations

2. Prepare Documents

  • 1-page CV
  • Professional Email
  • Writing Sample (optional)
  • LinkedIn Profile

3. Best Platforms

  • Lawctopus
  • LinkedIn
  • Telegram Groups
  • Firm Websites
  • Cold Emails

Common Mistakes

  • Generic emails
  • 3-page CV
  • No subject line
  • Spelling mistakes

  • AZB & Partners
  • Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas
  • Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas
  • Khaitan & Co
  • Trilegal

What They Look For

  • Strong academics
  • Research skills
  • Drafting ability
  • Commercial awareness

Reality Check

Tier-1 firms are extremely competitive. Networking, internships and strong practical skills matter more than certificates.

High Court Internships
Learn how court internships actually work.

What You’ll Do

  • Legal research
  • Case summaries
  • Court observation
  • Draft preparation

Skills Required

  • Bare Act reading
  • Research skills
  • Professional conduct

Important Reality

Court internships involve long observation hours and repetitive work initially, but they are valuable for litigation exposure.

Resume Tips for Law Students
Build a proper legal CV that recruiters actually read.

Golden Rule

Keep your CV one page only.

Include These Sections

  • Education
  • Internships
  • Moot Courts
  • Publications
  • Skills

Avoid These Mistakes

  • Fancy colors
  • Fake internships
  • School achievements
  • Unnecessary objectives
